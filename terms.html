1. These terms

These terms govern your use of Whisper Translate Offline. By installing or using the app you accept them. If you do not accept them, please do not use the app.

2. What Whisper Translate Offline does

Whisper Translate Offline records audio or opens an audio file you choose, transcribes it with the Whisper speech model, and can translate the transcript.

Everything runs on your device. The app has no server of its own and never sends your audio or your text anywhere.

3. Your content stays yours

You keep every right you have in the audio you record or import and in the text produced from it. We claim no licence over any of it.

Recordings are deleted as soon as transcription finishes. Only the transcript and its translation are saved, and only on your device.

4. Privacy

There are no accounts, no analytics and no telemetry. Nothing you record, type or translate is reported to us or to anyone else.

The only network request the app makes is the translation model download described below, and it happens only because you asked for it.

5. Downloads

The speech recognition model is included in the app. The translation model is about 1 GB and is downloaded once from the source named in Settings, then checked for integrity before it is used.

A download uses your data plan. The app checks for free space first and tells you if there is not enough, rather than failing partway through.

6. Accuracy

Automatic transcription and translation are not perfect. They can drop words, render names and technical terms wrongly, and produce fluent text that is simply not what was said — most often with poor audio, strong accents, overlapping speakers or specialised vocabulary.

Check anything that matters before you rely on it.
